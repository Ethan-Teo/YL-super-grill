Flip patties, stack burgers, and make rent. Every night gets busier.

How to play

Each night, cook for the customers at your counter and earn enough to pay rent.

  1. Grill. Tap the raw patties (or 🥩 Patty) to put one on the grill. Each patty costs $1.
  2. Flip. When ↻ appears over a patty, tap it. Both sides have to cook.
  3. Plate. When ✓ appears, tap the patty to move it onto your plate. Green is perfect; wait too long and it turns crispy, then burns.
  4. Top. Add the toppings shown on a ticket. Stacking order doesn't matter, the counts do.
  5. Serve. A ticket glows when your plate matches it. Tap the ticket or ring the 🔔 bell.

Walkouts and wrong orders cost a heart. The run ends when your hearts run out or you can't pay rent. After each night, pick an upgrade.
